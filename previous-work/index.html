An Observer-Based Vibration Suppression Method for Backlash-Induced Oscillations in EV Powertrains

Manato Kawasaki, Noboru Sakamoto, Akira Nakashima, Yasuhiko Kobayashi, Yutaro Tanaka
IEEE Access, Vol. 14, 2026, pp. 57444-57462 · DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3674637

Vehicle-test evidence reported in the paper

≈2000 → ≈1000 Nm
Peak driveshaft torsional torque: uncontrolled → AVDC / AVDIMC
1.41 → 0.57 / 0.53 s
Vibration settling time: uncontrolled → AVDC / AVDIMC

This predecessor study developed observer-based vibration suppression using AVDC and AVDIMC and validated the control framework in real-vehicle experiments. The current IFAC study focuses on a higher-accuracy nonlinear observer for backlash-state estimation.
